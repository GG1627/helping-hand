## Decisions that close the earlier audit

- **Promoted:** MAGNETIC and HAPTIC-STUDY, because the report should explain
  the scientific basis and testing needs of the planned orientation and haptic
  work. FIREBASE-PRIVACY is selected for the required networking description.
- **Added:** TFLITE-FLUTTER. The October 28 pubspec pins version 0.12.1;
  its versioned official package documentation identifies TensorFlow as the
  publisher and documents phone inference and isolate support. This closes
  the scope gap in using a microcontroller guide for the phone path.
- **Consolidated:** the separate internal model, integration, pilot, demo,
  stakeholder, test-plan, and review citations remain evidence links in the
  audit. REPO is the selected general artifact pointer. Direct use of a
  distinct prior report or borrowed figure would still require attribution.
- **Not selected for the intended draft:** the quaternion-specific activity
  paper, magnetic-fusion implementation tutorials, seller piezo listing,
  unrelated-part driver guidance, global e-waste report, IEEE ethics-code
  citation, separate Git/pytest/scaler references, and extra Flutter testing
  documentation. Their specific claims/tool exposition will not be carried
  into the planned prose. Preserve them in the audit rather than deleting
  the historical record. Retain ethical analysis and environmental impact
  discussion through project-specific reasoning and relevant selected sources.
- **Firebase detail:** the general integration and privacy sources are selected.
  The separate authentication and Firestore-rule pages were also rechecked
  November 12 and remain reserve sources. Add them if drafting goes into
  their specific API/rule details. Our own account/progress behavior should
  be described directly, without claiming independently verified isolation.
- **Scope control:** describe the orientation upgrade in plain language;
  a detailed quaternion-transform method was not implemented at the cutoff
  and is unnecessary for this preliminary report. Explain haptic testing
  needs without inventing drive ratings, completed cues, or learning gains.

## Verification and use limits

All selected external entries were checked on November 12 during this
reference review. Checks used official documentation, publisher text,
author-hosted papers, or primary-paper archives. For HAPTIC-STUDY, the
available publisher abstract supports the intended qualitative claim;
restricted full-text methods were not inspected, so do not add numerical
effects or detailed protocol claims from that study.

The MAGNETIC author manuscript explicitly identifies its journal version;
use the journal DOI above. The TCN source remains identified as a preprint.
The educator paper's citation block, author list, and study participation
were checked in the author PDF. Publication dates precede the cutoff.

The repository commit and its report/demo-note paths were inspected locally
with Git. That archive points to project artifacts; it is not independent
validation of our results or proof of which build ran during a demonstration.
The pinned tflite_flutter version was checked in that same October snapshot.

Documentation explains tools and protocols; it does not prove our installed
versions, measured throughput, security deployment, certification, or sensor
performance. The BLE primer is explanatory; NUS is a custom vendor service;
I2C UM10204 is a protocol specification. Distinguish these in the report.
Reference KiCad 9.0 as a documentation edition, not a confirmed team release.

Access dates are November 12 even though project results stop October 28.
The final bibliography should contain only sources actually used and should
have one entry per source, matched to every in-text number. Do not paste
draft IDs, verification notes, or this planning commentary into the report.
