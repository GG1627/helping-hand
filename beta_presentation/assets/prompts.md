# Optional image-generation prompts

Generated artwork is conceptual decoration, not hardware, recognition, or testing evidence. Images should contain no text; add captions in the slides.

## Learning problem

Create a clean editorial illustration for a technical presentation about learning American Sign Language. A learner sits at a desk studying a printed hand-sign reference and trying to compare their own hand position with the page, with an expression of concentration and mild uncertainty. The reference page should be abstract and indistinct, without a readable or instructional sign diagram. Use warm ivory (#FFFCF5), dark green (#183B36), teal (#007A70), and mint (#C8F0DD), restrained flat shapes, and ample empty space. Landscape 16:9 composition, with the subject concentrated on the right half so slide text can sit on the left. No text, logos, speech bubbles, or invented instructional ASL poses. This is a conceptual illustration about independent learning, not an ASL lesson.
